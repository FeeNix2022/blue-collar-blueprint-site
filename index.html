FAQ

      
Short answers


      

        What is the Revenue Leak Audit?
        
A written review of how many calls you miss, what they likely cost, and where in your intake process jobs are slipping. It is free.

      

      

        Do I need new software or a new phone number?
        
The audit does not require either. What setup looks like for the text-back system depends on your phone provider, and we cover that in the audit.

      

      

        Will customers know it’s automated?
        
The first text is automated and says so plainly. You or your staff take over once the caller replies with a job.

      

      

        How accurate is the calculator?
        
It is a ceiling, not a forecast. It assumes every missed call would have closed at your normal rate. Actual recovery will be lower. The audit replaces those assumptions with your real numbers.

      

      

        Who runs this?
        
Blue Collar Blueprint is a division of Phoenix Heart AI.
